Original narrative gouache illustration study for an intimate birthday garden. Landscape 3:2 composition. A small warm ivory fluffy round light-being with just two tiny ear-like tufts, no human limbs, no realistic rabbit anatomy, is watching an ivory folded paper bird tug the loose corner of a piece of wrapping tissue. Beneath the tissue is a small plain sage-green closed passport-sized folio, lying low on the ground, partly revealed. The tissue is taut between bird and gift: this gentle unexpected action is the story. These three small subjects together occupy about the lower central third of the painting, never a product closeup. An expansive quiet blue-grey and muted sage painted ground surrounds them, without a horizon or scenery. One restrained diagonal patch of warm pale light connects them and creates a soft common contact shadow. Opaque dry gouache, broad irregular brush strokes, scumbled pigment, softened lost edges, deliberately simple shapes and a little quiet humor; varied paint surface but generous undisturbed space. Palette: muted slate blue, misty sage, warm ivory. Gift rendered as a few imperfect painted planes, no leather pores, fine stitching, logos, text or product-render gloss. Neither photorealism nor a plush-toy commercial. No decorative stars, glitter, plants, borders, card frames, interface, typography, butterflies, floral arrangements or extra props. Full painted background; no white cutout. This is a standalone composition study, not a finished webpage.
