## A — Quiet unboxing

Reference inputs:
- public/art/gifts/rest-gift-box-v1.png
- public/art/gifts/sleep-mask-cutout-v2.png

Use case: compositing / illustration-story.
Create concept A for a quiet interactive gift-opening scene in a hand-painted secret garden website. Recompose the two reference objects into one NEW finished scene, rather than presenting isolated product cutouts. Reference 1 gives the identity of the ivory rectangular box and sage silk ribbon; reference 2 gives the dusty blush padded sleep mask with gathered elastic strap. Preserve these identities but harmonize every surface with a beautiful original gouache-and-watercolor illustration.
A single gently elevated three-quarter view onto warm ivory textured paper. In the lower middle of the picture, a shallow open ivory gift box rests physically on the surface; its lid is laid slightly behind and to the left, its loosened sage ribbon softly trails over the nearby surface. The blush mask is nestled INSIDE the box, following its perspective, at its true physical scale, with one lower corner and a little of its strap obscured by two irregular folds of fine translucent cream tissue. The front wall of the box occludes the tissue and lower edge of the mask. The other tissue folds softly spill over the box sides. This should feel like a gift that someone has just opened, not merchandise hovering in the center. One tiny blank folded ivory tag attached to ribbon, understated.
Compose a single lovely small visual vignette occupying about 58 percent of image width and the lower 60 percent of height, with generous breathing space above and asymmetric balance. The open box is foreground-center-right, detached lid behind-left creating spatial depth. Broad very faint sage wash around lower corners fades into the ivory ground; no scene border, no rectilinear UI card. Painterly edge variation, visible paper grain, gentle contact shadows under the box and ribbon, soft translucent paper and tactile cloth. Light from upper left. Soft but clearly legible forms and focal contrast, not uniformly pale or washed out. Muted sage, warm ivory, dusky rose, no dramatic spotlight.
This is A: entirely still, elegant physical unboxing, no character.
No text, typography, labels, button, product name, diagram, moon, stars, sparkles, extra gifts, flowers, furniture, photorealism, 3D plastic render, geometric vector folds, thick outlines, frame or watermark. Landscape composition, high quality concept artwork.

## B — Resting companion

Reference input: generated concept A.

Use case: compositing / illustration-story.
Edit this concept image into variation B, a quiet story moment at a rest stop in a secret garden. Keep the SAME ivory shallow open box, blush pink sleep mask resting inside it, textured tissue folds, detached lid behind-left, sage ribbon and tiny blank tag, with consistent perspective and a single unified watercolor/gouache medium. Keep the gift clearly for the viewer: the creature must not wear or occupy the mask.
Improve the composition to give more air and narrative context: pull the camera back so the complete arrangement is centered in the lower-middle 70 percent of the scene width, with more surrounding warm ivory paper surface. Mask remains the largest focal shape, nesting at a gentle angle inside the box and partly overlapped by tissue and its front wall.
Add exactly ONE existing garden companion nestled against the outside front-left corner of the gift box, beside the trailing ribbon: a small cream fluffy softly luminous ball, about one quarter the width of the mask. It has NO face, NO eyes, NO mouth, NO limbs, NO clothing. Its very soft subtly furry silhouette is slightly flattened and leaned to one side, visibly settled onto the surface for a nap, with a tiny tucked bump in its outline, rather than a perfect circle. Its cream tuft edges and broad grounded contact shadow convey a small warm being resting, not a flashlight or spark. It has the same handmade brush texture as everything else. Its glow is subtle, not overexposed or surrounded by magic particles.
Suggest an unhurried afternoon: a softly feathered pale sage dappled shadow crosses the otherwise quiet ivory surface diagonally. Only light and color, no painted leaves in the scene. The mask, box and character are crisp enough to read; quiet background. Restrained affection and a little gentle humor from the sleepy small shape beside the waiting present, no sentimental copy.
No additional objects, furniture, flowers, bedroom, star field, moon, particles, confetti, floating objects, caption, lettering, UI frame, product cards, watermark. Single landscape illustration. Do not turn this into a split image or storyboard.

## B — Three-frame storyboard

Reference inputs:
- generated concept B
- public/art/gifts/rest-gift-box-v1.png

Use case: illustration-story / storyboard.
Using reference image 1 as the exact FINISHED scene and reference image 2 only to understand the closed gift box, produce a carefully composed THREE-FRAME horizontal storyboard of the SAME gift opening. Three equal landscape-ish panels side by side, separated by slender blank cream gutters, on one wide 3:1 illustration sheet. No visible panel borders. No words, numbers, labels, arrows or interface controls.
Keep the box, mask, paper, ribbon, sleepy fluffy companion, illustration style and soft dappled sage light absolutely consistent with reference 1. All three frames use the SAME elevated three-quarter camera and fixed spatial framing, with box BASE stationary in lower-center-right, same size and same ground contact in all frames. The same box lid changes position; never duplicate a lid or add a second box. Soft original gouache/watercolor painting, ivory grain, sage ribbon, dusty blush mask; coherent physical perspective and clear occlusion.
LEFT FRAME — unopened: the ivory box is closed, with sage ribbon bow across the lid as in reference 2. Contents are completely concealed. The small faceless creamy fluffy orb companion is round and upright at the front-left of the box, waiting beside it. The box and companion rest on the surface with shadows. No extra detached lid anywhere.
MIDDLE FRAME — opening in progress: the one lid has tilted off and moved to its resting position behind-left, still a little raised at one edge. Ribbon loosens naturally, its tail following the lid. Two broad soft tissue folds are parting outward from the center of the box; a clearly recognizable but only partly revealed blush upper edge of the sleep mask is visible inside. Mask does not float, move out, or grow in size. Companion leans toward the box, shorter and gently tilted, still outside. This frame should read as physically unpacking paper, not an exploding gift or teleport effect.
RIGHT FRAME — settled: faithfully reproduce the physical final arrangement in reference 1: detached lid lies behind-left, mask rests nested in crumpled cream tissue INSIDE the box, folded tissue spills over its sides, sage ribbon trails left to its tiny blank tag, companion has settled flatter and slightly leaned against the outside front-left corner. This is a nap implied only by body posture; it has no face, limbs, accessories, letters or sleep symbols. All items remain visible in a quiet balanced little scene.
The size of the creature is about one quarter of the mask width. It does not wear the mask or get inside the gift. Strong readable difference between the opening states. Generous empty warm ivory space above each small arrangement. Final art, not rough pencil diagrams. No moon, stars, sparkle, confetti, furniture, flowers, extra animals, captions, frames, UI panels, product names, watermark, photoreal rendering, flat vector geometry. Visually consistent wide storyboard.
