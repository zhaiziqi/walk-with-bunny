## Proposed sequence

2. Lid opens; a visibly distinct little stream of irregular warm dots and a few painted star marks rises from within. Mask is still mostly concealed.
3. Dots thin and drift away; simplified dusty-pink mask is revealed in the open box. Existing faceless fluffy companion catches the warm light.
